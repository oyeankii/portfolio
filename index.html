UX REDESIGN BRIEF
Indite.io
AI automation & chatbot platform · Full product redesign
PROBLEM STATEMENT
Indite.io offers powerful AI automation, chatbot creation, and agent marketplace capabilities — but its current interface overwhelms users with dense information, a cluttered UI, and no clear onboarding path. Non-technical business users and operators struggle to understand where to start, how to build, and how to navigate the platform — leading to high cognitive load, low feature adoption, and user drop-off before they experience core value.
GOALS
Reduce Cognitive LoadSimplify the interface by removing visual clutter and restructuring information hierarchy so users can focus on one task at a time.
Clear Navigation PathsGuide users from entry to value with structured onboarding and contextual wayfinding — removing dead ends and ambiguous flows.
Establish a Design SystemBuild a scalable component library with tokens, variables, and patterns that create visual and functional consistency.
Simplify WorkflowsAbstract n8n-level complexity into digestible, guided steps — making automation accessible without removing power.
SOLUTION
Progressive DisclosureShow only what's relevant at each step. Hide advanced settings behind expandable panels. Surface complexity only when users are ready.
Guided Onboarding FlowA step-by-step experience that helps users set up their first chatbot or automation in under 5 minutes, with templates as starting points.
Restructured IAReorganise the platform into clear sections: Build, Deploy, Monitor, and Marketplace — with consistent navigation and logical groupings.
Design System From ScratchDefine tokens, components, and patterns using Figma variables — scalable, consistent design that speeds up future dev handoff.

KEY RESEARCH QUESTIONS
1. Where do new users get stuck first?

Users land on a dense dashboard with no clear call to action. They see chatbot, automation, and agent marketplace all at once, with no hierarchy telling them where to start. The first question becomes "what do I do first?" — and most won't find the answer.

2. Which features do users prioritise?

Most non-technical users come to Indite with a specific, tangible goal: "I want a chatbot for my website." It's the most concrete and familiar use case.

3. What mental model do users arrive with?

They expect a customer support tool — a chat widget they can embed and configure simply. They get confused when they see automation builders and agent marketplaces.

4. How do users describe the platform?

"It's a platform where you build AI chatbots for your business, automate repetitive tasks, and hire pre-built AI agents — without needing to code."

5. What does success look like in session one?

Varies from user to user — which is exactly why guided, persona-aware onboarding matters.
